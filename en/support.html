Support

Last updated: 22 September 2026

A question, a bug, or a card the app cannot read? Here is where to start.

Getting in touch

The simplest route is to open an issue on GitHub. Describe what you expected and what happened instead; if it concerns a card that was misread, mention the kind of card (dark stock, gold foil, handwriting…) without attaching anyone’s contact details.

Requirements

Comlynk runs on iPhone, from iOS 26 onwards.

Frequently asked questions

The app misreads a card

Recognition depends a great deal on light and contrast. Lay the card flat on a plain surface, avoid reflections on foil and varnish, and frame it tightly. Every field stays editable: a quick correction beats a second scan.

Where are my cards stored?

On your iPhone only, in the app’s private storage. Nothing is sent over the internet. See the privacy policy.

Can I move my cards to another iPhone?

The data follows your iPhone backup. Restoring a backup onto a new device brings your records back with the app. There is no sync between devices, since there is no account.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app: iOS wipes all of its data at the same time. To remove a single record, delete it from the list.

Does the app work without a network?

Yes. Text recognition happens on the device. Only showing an address on a map needs a connection.
